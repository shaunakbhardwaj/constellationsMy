## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy establishes the framework for allocating, approving, and monitoring budgets for strategic initiatives. It applies to all projects with budgets exceeding $500,000 or spanning more than two fiscal quarters.

All department heads, project leads, and financial coordinators must adhere to this policy when proposing, managing, or reporting on strategic initiatives.

## 3. Budget Request Process

### 3.1 Submission Requirements

All Tier 1 and Tier 2 initiatives must submit:

1. **Project Brief** (Template: FIN-TEMPLATE-001)
   - Executive summary
   - Business case with ROI analysis
   - Resource requirements
   - Risk assessment

2. **Detailed Budget Breakdown**
   - Personnel costs (fully loaded)
   - Infrastructure and tools
   - Third-party services
   - Contingency allocation (minimum 5%)

3. **Milestone Schedule**
   - Phase definitions
   - Key deliverables per phase
   - Budget release triggers

4. **Steering Committee Composition**
   - Executive sponsor
   - Finance representative
   - Key stakeholder representation

### 3.2 Review Timeline

| Tier | Initial Review | Final Decision | Total Timeline |
|------|----------------|----------------|----------------|
| Tier 1 | 10 business days | 15 business days | 25 business days |
| Tier 2 | 5 business days | 10 business days | 15 business days |
| Tier 3 | 3 business days | 5 business days | 8 business days |

## 4. Budget Controls

### 4.1 Variance Thresholds

Project leads must request formal approval for:
- Any single line item exceeding approved budget by 10%
- Cumulative variance exceeding 5% of total project budget
- Any scope changes affecting bottom-line budget

**Example:** Project Alpha's original IT infrastructure allocation was $300,000. When cloud costs were projected to reach $345,000 (15% over), Sarah Chen submitted variance request FIN-VAR-2024-047, which was approved by Linda Zhao on October 15, 2024.

### 4.2 Monthly Reporting Requirements

All active initiatives must submit by the 5th of each month:
- Actual spend vs. budget by category
- Forecast for remaining project duration
- Explanation of variances >5%
- Updated risk register

Reports should be submitted to finance-initiatives@company.com with copy to the executive sponsor.

### 4.3 Quarterly Reviews

Finance conducts quarterly deep-dives with all Tier 1 project leads:

## 5. Budget Modifications

### 5.1 Reallocation Within Project

Project leads may reallocate up to 10% between budget categories without approval if:
- Total project budget remains unchanged
- Core deliverables are unaffected
- Change is documented in monthly report

### 5.2 Additional Funding Requests

Requests for budget increases must include:
- Root cause analysis for the overrun
- Impact assessment if additional funding denied
- Proposed mitigation for future phases
- Updated ROI calculation

**Recent approvals:**
- Project Alpha contingency increase: +$50,000 (approved October 20, 2024)
- Data Platform scope addition: +$180,000 (approved September 14, 2024)

### 5.3 Budget Release

Funds are released in tranches aligned with project milestones:

| Project | Phase 1 | Phase 2 | Phase 3 | Total |
|---------|---------|---------|---------|-------|
| Project Alpha | $800,000 | $1,000,000 | $600,000 | $2,400,000 |
| Infrastructure Mod | $1,200,000 | $1,400,000 | $500,000 | $3,100,000 |
| Global Expansion | $1,500,000 | $2,000,000 | $1,300,000 | $4,800,000 |

Phase 2 funding for any project requires:
- Phase 1 deliverables completed and accepted
- Variance within 10% of Phase 1 budget
- Steering committee sign-off

## 9. Exception Process

In extraordinary circumstances, expedited approval may be granted:
- Competitive response requirement
- Customer commitment at risk
- Regulatory compliance mandate

Exception requests go directly to Linda Zhao and require CEO notification within 48 hours. All exceptions are reported to the Board Audit Committee quarterly.

## 10. Policy Compliance

### 10.1 Non-Compliance Consequences

Failure to adhere to this policy may result in:
- Budget freeze for the initiative
- Required remediation plan
- Escalation to executive sponsor
- Inclusion in performance review (for project leads)

### 10.2 Training Requirements

All new project leads must complete "Strategic Initiative Financial Management" training (Course ID: FIN-101) within 30 days of assignment. Annual refresher training required for all active project leads.
