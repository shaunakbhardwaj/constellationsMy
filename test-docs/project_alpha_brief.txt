**Document Classification:** Internal - Confidential

**Executive Sponsor:** Marcus Thompson, VP of Product

---

## Executive Summary

Project Alpha represents our flagship initiative to modernize the customer onboarding experience. This $2.4M investment aims to reduce customer time-to-value from 14 days to under 3 days while maintaining our 94% satisfaction rating.

Sarah Chen has been appointed as Project Lead, bringing her experience from the successful Velocity platform migration. She will work closely with the Engineering, Design, and Customer Success teams to deliver Phase 1 by end of Q4.

---

## Background and Context

Following the Q3 customer feedback analysis conducted by Jennifer Martinez's research team, we identified significant friction points in our onboarding flow:

1. **Documentation Overload**: New customers receive 47 pages of setup documentation
2. **Manual Configuration**: 23 configuration steps require human intervention
3. **Delayed Activation**: Average 14-day gap between contract signing and first meaningful usage

The competitive analysis by David Park showed that newcomers like Streamline.io are achieving 24-hour activation rates. Without intervention, we risk losing market share to these faster-moving competitors.

---

## Project Scope

### Phase 1: Automated Setup Wizard (Q4 2024)
- Self-service configuration portal
- AI-powered default recommendations
- Integration health checks
- **Budget:** $800,000
- **Team:** 5 engineers, 2 designers, 1 PM

### Phase 2: Guided Onboarding Experience (Q1 2025)
- Interactive product tours
- Contextual help system
- Success milestone tracking
- **Budget:** $1,000,000
- **Team:** 8 engineers, 3 designers, 2 PMs

### Phase 3: Predictive Success Indicators (Q2 2025)
- Usage pattern analysis
- Churn risk prediction
- Automated intervention workflows
- **Budget:** $600,000
- **Team:** 4 engineers, 1 data scientist
